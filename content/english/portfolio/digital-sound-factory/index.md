**Role:** Web Developer (Part of Vision Init Self 2004-2013)

**Overview:** Developed and launched the original e-commerce website for Digital Sound Factory in 2007, a company established by E-mu Systems veteran Timothy Swartz to provide high-quality, license-free audio sound downloads for computer software synthesizers.

**Key Contributions:**

*   **E-commerce Implementation:** Built the initial online store using the OsCommerce platform.
*   **Site Launch:** Successfully delivered the first version of the site, enabling online sales of digital audio products.
*   Supported a new venture in the music technology space.
