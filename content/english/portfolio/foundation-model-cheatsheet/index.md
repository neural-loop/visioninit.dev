## Project: Foundation Model Cheatsheet Website

As part of the community effort fostered through AI Models, we built a new online platform from scratch at [fmcheatsheet.org](https://fmcheatsheet.org) for the Foundation Model Development Cheatsheet, collaborating with the Cheatsheet team. This comprehensive resource promotes responsible AI development practices.

**Role:** Collaborator / Web Developer (via AI Models initiative)

**Key Features of the New Website:**

1.  **Improved User Experience:** Replaced the previous Streamlit app with a more intuitive, user-friendly Hugo-based site.
2.  **Enhanced Searchability:** Enabled easier navigation and discovery of specific resources within the extensive catalog.
3.  **SEO Optimization:** Built the website with growth in mind, improving discoverability.
4.  **Comprehensive Resource Catalog:** Provides access to over 250 tools and resources for responsible AI development.
5.  **Interactive Elements:** Offers more direct interaction compared to traditional academic survey papers.

The Foundation Model Development Cheatsheet is a collaborative effort by researchers from multiple leading institutions, serving as a guide for ethical AI development covering data sourcing, model training, evaluation, risk mitigation, and environmental impact.

We invite the AI community to explore the website at [fmcheatsheet.org](https://fmcheatsheet.org).
