## Project: AI Models (aimodels.org)

**Role:** Founder & Lead Developer (2021 - 2024)

**Overview:** Architected and developed aimodels.org using Hugo, creating a scalable platform focused on open-source AI models. Managed all aspects of development, server administration (including Google Cloud), and implemented robust SEO strategies, culminating in a successful acquisition in 2024.

**Key Contributions:**

*   Strategic development and growth leading to acquisition.
*   Significant organic traffic increase via SEO.
*   Fostered community collaboration through related sites like [open-neuromorphic.org](https://open-neuromorphic.org/) and [fmcheatsheet.org](https://fmcheatsheet.org/).
*   Managed full project lifecycle from conception to deployment and maintenance.
*   Utilized cloud services like Google Cloud Run, Cloud Build, and Cloud SQL.
