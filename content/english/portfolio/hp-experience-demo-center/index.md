## Project: HP Experience Demonstration Center (via Design Reactor)

**Role:** Web Developer (Part of Vision Init Self 2004-2013)

**Overview:** Developed the backend content management interface for the HP Experience Demonstration Center. This facility used interactive touch screens to provide clients and influencers with a journey through HP's capabilities.

**Key Contributions:**

*   **Backend Interface Development:** Produced the interface allowing staff to manage the content displayed on the demonstration center's interactive screens.
*   **Content Management:** Enabled dynamic updates for collaboration rooms, capability demonstrations, and tours presented through the interactive system.
*   Supported HP's goal of showcasing innovation and processes to clients and prospects.
