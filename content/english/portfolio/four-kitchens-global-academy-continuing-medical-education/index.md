## Project: Global Academy for Continuing Medical Education (via Four Kitchens)

**Role:** Drupal Developer (Contract, 2017)

**Overview:** Engaged by Four Kitchens, a digital agency, to provide development support for the Global Academy for Continuing Medical Education website ([globalacademycme.com](https://www.globalacademycme.com/)). This role involved contributing key features and ensuring stability for the site launch.

**Key Contributions:**

*   **Launch Support:** Provided additional development push toward the completion and launch of the website.
*   **Migration Feature:** Produced a feature to enable the migration of complex 'micro-site' pages into Drupal content with minimal downtime.
*   **Testing:** Provided test coverage utilizing Behat.
*   **Collaboration:** Worked with internal Four Kitchens teams and clients to translate business requirements into scalable technical solutions.
*   Addressed backlog items and implemented fixes.
