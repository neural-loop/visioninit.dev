## Project: HP & DVF Experience Lounge (via Design Reactor)

**Role:** Web Developer (Part of Vision Init Self 2004-2013)

**Overview:** Developed backend systems for a high-profile, three-day multi-media event collaboration between HP and Diane von Furstenberg, celebrating Fashion’s Night Out. The project involved creating interfaces for managing content displayed on interactive screens during the event.

**Key Contributions:**

*   **Backend Development:** Launched backend products/interfaces enabling content entry and consumption by interactive touch screens used at the event.
*   **Configurability:** Designed solutions with ease of maintenance and configurability, allowing them to become standard offerings for Design Reactor.
*   Supported a unique digital experience blending fashion and technology.
