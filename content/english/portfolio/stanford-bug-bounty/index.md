## Project: Stanford Graduate School of Business Website

**Role:** Senior Web Developer (2017 - 2020)

**Overview:** Contributed significantly to the development and enhancement of the Stanford Graduate School of Business web presence. This involved full-stack development within the Drupal ecosystem, close collaboration with marketing teams, and a focus on performance and security.

**Key Contributions:**

*   **Custom Development:** Extensive experience in developing custom Drupal modules, themes, and distributions using PHP and Drupal APIs.
*   **Migration Support:** Supported the migration from Drupal 7 to Drupal 9, including tasks like Salesforce integration and migrating content types into bundles.
*   **Site Redesign & Features:** Partnered with marketing to shape the site towards storytelling through a site redesign and the implementation of the 'Voices' functionality.
*   **Performance & Caching:** Refactored code in multiple areas to improve responsiveness and applied appropriate caching strategies.
*   **Security Contributions:** Actively participated in Stanford's Bug Bounty program, identifying and reporting several vulnerabilities (~$1500 awarded), enhancing organizational security posture. ($500 also awarded separately for huggingface.co vulnerability report).
*   **Collaboration & Process:** Worked effectively with release engineers and frontend developers; maintained the Linux development build and onboarded colleagues/contractors.
