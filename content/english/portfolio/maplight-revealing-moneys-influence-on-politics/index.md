## Project: MapLight - Revealing Money's Influence on Politics

**Role:** Drupal Developer (2013 - 2015)

**Overview:** Applied Software Development Lifecycle knowledge to develop and maintain Drupal sites ([maplight.org](https://maplight.org) and votersedge.org) for MapLight, a non-profit organization focused on political transparency. The role involved architecting data-driven solutions and ensuring high performance.

**Key Contributions:**

*   **Drupal Site Development:** Developed Drupal sites, including custom module development and theming.
*   **Enterprise Architecture:** Utilized Drupal modules to architect enterprise solutions encompassing data management, display logic, and information architecture.
*   **Availability Monitoring:** Spearheaded availability monitoring by creating synthetic tests (using Selenium) which identified several performance issues.
*   **Performance Optimization:** Ensured high performance utilizing tools like New Relic and Blazemeter.
*   **DevOps Support:** Supported configuration management of tools like JIRA, Confluence, and Bamboo.
