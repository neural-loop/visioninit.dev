## Project: Homeland Security Enterprise - Geospatial Concept of Operations (via ArdentMC)

**Role:** Senior CMS Applications Developer (Consulting, 2015 - 2016)

**Overview:** Leveraged deep expertise in Drupal 7 full-stack development to build complex web applications, themes, and plugins for the Geospatial Concept of Operations (GeoCONOPS) portal, part of the federal GeoPlatform initiative. This project aimed to enhance geospatial data sharing and collaboration within the Homeland Security Enterprise (HSE).

**Key Contributions:**

*   **Full-Stack Drupal Development:** Built and maintained complex Drupal 7 components for the GeoCONOPS platform.
*   **Project Delivery:** Coordinated efforts using FogBugz issue tracking to streamline project delivery and enhancements within an expedited contract window.
*   **Environment Transition:** Transitioned efforts towards a virtual environment, significantly improving response and update times.
*   **Technical Communication:** Translated and communicated complex technical challenges into clear and concise forms for non-developer stakeholders.
*   Emphasized principles of Open Government through support for open formats and data standards inherent in the GeoPlatform.
